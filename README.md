### ṚTA — the flag on this profile

*Ṛta* is the Vedic principle of **order, truth, and right correspondence between action
and consequence.** It is exactly what a proof is: the consequence follows from the
action by rule, and no other outcome is possible.

Most verification products answer *does this look right to you*. ṚTA answers the
stronger question — **does this follow, by rule, with no alternative?** The buyer
proves *balance ≥ 1000* and sends only the proof. The verifier learns the threshold is
met and never learns the balance. That is why it does not need to trust the buyer.
